# Changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-07

First version.

- Type a scene and Overtone writes music whose spectrogram paints it: people act out what you describe (eating, throwing, kicking, dancing, arguing, flying and more), with costumes, kids and groups.
- Unknown names, places and works are looked up on Wikipedia, using freely licensed pictures only, credited in each video.
- Everyday things are traced from the bundled Noto Emoji font (SIL Open Font License).
- Each prompt gets its own key, tempo, chords, bass, drums and melody; Remix rolls a new arrangement.
- Paint images and videos as well as text.
- Record video: 1080p MP4 in 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16, about 0.6 MB per second, tested in Chrome and Safari. WAV export and a copyable caption.
- Prompt and audio checks in tests/ (55 passing), a double-click Mac launcher, MIT license.
